# 15, Android developer verification and the app stores

Not built. Stub.

What it takes for the app to reach people beyond GitHub releases and
Obtainium, which plan 11 covers. Every part of it exchanges a person's
identity with Google or another store, so a person does it by hand.

## Decided

- The app is registered with Android's developer verification before
  it is enforced worldwide in 2027.
- Google Play and F-Droid come later, if outside interest makes them
  worth it.

## Open

- Whether the app is verified under a personal account or an
  organization's.
- For Google Play: Play App Signing with the release key from plan 11
  as the upload key, or a new upload key, and the closed test with 12
  testers for 14 days that a new personal account needs first.
- For F-Droid: a reproducible build, and a flavor without Firebase,
  since F-Droid's main repository takes no proprietary libraries.
